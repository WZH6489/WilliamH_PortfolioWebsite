SFU SideQuests

A gamified campus companion. Pickable side-quests across social, academic, fitness and exploration tracks — designed for the time between classes.

  • Figma
  • UI/UX Design
  • Canva
  • Git
View on Devpost ↗

Inspiration

A common complaint about university life is how monotonous the in-between hours can feel. SideQuests was designed so students have small things to do between classes — a way to engage with their community, the campus, and the city around SFU.

What it does

Quests sit across social, academic, fitness and exploration tracks, with a custom “Add Quest” loop so students can contribute their own.

How we built it

The app was prototyped end-to-end in Figma during a 12-hour build window, with Canva for marketing assets and Git for shared notes. We then delivered a comprehensive project pitch to a panel of industry experts, communicating the technical architecture, design philosophy, and user engagement strategy.

My role

Team of three (Vincent Zhao, Justin Tan, me). I built the full Figma prototype — quest picker, add-a-quest flow, profile screen, and navigation between them. Used Canva for pitch slides. I presented the demo to the judge panel; teammates contributed quest content and user research notes.

What we learned

Faster Figma flows, better Git hygiene, and how to scope something so it actually finishes inside the clock.
