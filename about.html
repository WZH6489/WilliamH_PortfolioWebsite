02 — Principles

How I work

Quiet by default

The interface should disappear. I prefer few moving parts, honest typography, and decisions you don’t notice until you need them.

Finish things

A shipped 80% beats a perfect 100% in a draft folder. I optimise for things that survive a Monday morning.

Read the room

Good engineering is mostly empathy — for users, teammates, and the person who has to maintain this in six months.
